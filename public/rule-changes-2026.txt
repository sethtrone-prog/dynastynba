2026-2027 Changes

Rookie Draft Lottery Changes

SECTION 5 — ROOKIE DRAFT & LOTTERY

5.1 The Rookie Draft has two rounds and uses the same order in both rounds (not a snake draft).

5.2 The six non-playoff franchises each choose a distinct NBA lottery team, in this fantasy standings order: fifth place chooses first, then sixth through tenth place.

5.3 The actual NBA lottery ranking among ONLY the six selected NBA teams determines fantasy Rookie Draft positions 1–6. Unselected NBA teams are ignored for this purpose.

5.4 Playoff franchise draft slots: fourth place picks seventh; third place picks eighth; runner-up picks ninth; champion picks tenth.

5.5 Traded picks normally retain the original franchise's draft position. The missed-games penalty described in Section 2 instead applies to the next first-round selection the offending franchise actually makes, including an acquired pick; there is no sixth-pick exemption.

Playoff Eligibility Changes

SECTION 10 — PLAYOFFS & POSTSEASON ELIGIBILITY

10.1 The top four franchises qualify for a two-week postseason. Seeding ties are resolved by head-to-head record, then total fantasy points.

10.2 A tied playoff matchup is decided by the franchise with more points in the PTS scoring category.

10.3 Playoff rosters lock at playoff commencement. Standard free agency closes. G-League and Two-Way players cannot play unless promoted before the playoffs or approved as an injury replacement.

10.4 Playoff injury replacement requests may be submitted starting Sunday of the final regular-season matchup and must be submitted to the Commissioner and approved BEFORE playoffs begin.

10.5 The injured active player must have a verifiable injury designation, short-term or long-term, supported by an NBA injury report or reliable source. Rest, healthy scratches and suspensions do not qualify.

10.6 The replacement must come from the requesting franchise's own G-League or Two-Way roster. Approval requires a simple majority of actual yes/no votes cast by eligible franchises; the requesting franchise does not vote and abstentions are excluded.

10.7 Once replaced, the injured player is permanently removed from that franchise's active playoff roster even if the player recovers. The replacement must remain on the playoff roster for the entire postseason. Roster size, contracts, salary and future rights are unchanged.

10.8 The DPE is governed by the separate timing and eligibility provisions of Section 9.

Schedule changed to an 18 week regular season.

Lineup format changed to G, G, F, F, C, G/F, PF/C, UTL, UTL

Change stepien rule to read that you only need to have a pick in every

Changes to Tier Levels:
-A player must now play at least 50 games to qualify for Tier B.
-If a player earns All-NBA honors, they will retain that status for three seasons, unless they later play 62 or more games in a season without receiving All-NBA recognition. After the three-season window expires, the player’s tier will revert to their most recent assigned value, based on a season where they played over 50 games.

Buyout market removed from League Rules.

TRADE DEADLINE: The trade deadline is now moved to the Monday at 1pm following the NBA trade deadline.

A new Super Max Contract Extension in the length of 5 years will be added as an option. Only players who have been awarded All-NBA status in their career, and have a value 3 units or higher to sign are eligible. Players may not be traded within the first three years of this contract. Players may be traded anytime, within the current trade rules, in years 4 and 5 beginning 12/15 of year 4.

2024-2025 Changes
DPE Rule - If an NBA team is granted a DPE for a player then that player will be eligible in our league for a DPE. No other players will be eligible. You must use your DPE before the start of the Fantasy league playoffs.

We are introducing two new contract options. The first being a 4 year MAX contract. These are no longer considered SUPER MAX contracts. The eligibility for these contracts are any player with a value of 2 units or more (Tier B or Tier C with a +1 resigning penalty). Rookies coming off their first contact are eligible if they are Tier B. This will keep the RME eligibility for the next contract. These contracts cannot be traded in Year 1 of the contract and become eligible 12/15 of the second year. There is no maximum to how many 4 year contracts you have on your roster.

Starting next year (next offseason) a new Super Max Contract Extension in the length of 5 years will be added as an option. Only players who have been awarded All-NBA status in their career, and have a value 3 units or higher to sign are eligible. Players may not be traded within the first three years of this contract. Players may be traded anytime, within the current trade rules, in years 4 and 5 beginning 12/15 of year 4.

DeMar DeRozan rule - players sent out in trades are no longer able to be acquired by the original team via a trade for a 2 year period from the initial trade date.

Clarification for trading in the offseason is as follows. Rosters will be locked for me to complete off season work until 7 days before the rookie draft. You may negotiate trades at any time but they will not be approved until that date. That date is 7/22 this year.

I am open to allowing people to submit their free agency decisions at any time after 7/22 until the deadline, but once communicated they will be final.
