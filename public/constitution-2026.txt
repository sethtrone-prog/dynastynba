DYNASTY NBA LEAGUE CONSTITUTION

Founded 2018

A true franchise-building experience inspired by the NBA. Build, manage, and sustain a championship contender under a hard salary cap, with contracts, roster decisions, and strategic rules modeled on the NBA’s collective bargaining agreement.

SECTION 1 — LEAGUE OVERVIEW & SCORING

1.1 Dynasty NBA was founded in 2018 and consists of ten franchises competing in a head-to-head dynasty basketball format.

1.2 The regular season lasts 18 matchup weeks. Four franchises qualify for the playoffs.

1.3 Each franchise is subject to a hard salary cap of 25 Units, except for expressly stated salary exceptions.

1.4 Fantasy scoring: points scored +1; rebounds +0.75; assists +0.75; steals +1; blocks +1; turnovers -1; missed field goals -0.5; missed free throws -0.5.

SECTION 2 — ROSTERS & LINEUP REQUIREMENTS

2.1 Each franchise must maintain a minimum of 13 active players and may carry up to 15 active players, up to two injured reserve players, up to three Two-Way players during the regular season, and up to five G-League players. Maximum total roster size is 25.

2.2 The nine starting positions are G, G, F, F, C, G/F, PF/C, UTIL and UTIL.

2.3 Lineups may be adjusted daily. Individual players lock at the tipoff of their NBA game.

2.4 The normal weekly games limit is 27, subject to officially announced adjustments.

2.5 For each game above the weekly limit, points scored in the excess game are deducted, plus an additional 20 fantasy points and a $5 financial penalty per excess game.

2.6 For each game below the weekly limit, the franchise is fined $5. There are no exceptions to the missed-games fine.

2.7 A franchise accumulating ten missed games receives a one-position penalty on its next first-round Rookie Draft selection that it actually makes, including a traded-for pick. The sanction does not follow an original pick the franchise traded away. The penalized pick swaps position with the selection immediately behind it. There is no exception for the sixth lottery pick and no additional fifteen-game penalty.

2.8 Fewest missed games is a tiebreaker where expressly applicable elsewhere in this Constitution.

SECTION 3 — SALARY CAP & PLAYER TIERS

3.1 Tier A: 3 Units; approximately 15 All-NBA players as designated under league tiering rules.

3.2 Tier B: 2 Units; the next top 50 players in fantasy points per game, including ties, excluding Tier A and rookie-scale players.

3.3 Tier C: 1 Unit; remaining veteran players outside Tiers A and B.

3.4 Tier D: 1 Unit; a player's first four NBA seasons, structured as two guaranteed contract years and two team-option years.

3.5 Tier A injury protection: a player with 61.5 or fewer NBA games played may retain the previous tier at Commissioner discretion until exceeding 61.5 games played.

3.6 Tier B injury protection: a player with 50 or fewer NBA games played may retain the previous tier at Commissioner discretion until exceeding 50 games played.

SECTION 4 — CONTRACTS & EXTENSIONS

4.1 Normal contract lengths: Tier A, one to four years; Tier B, one to three years except expressly authorized special extensions; Tier C, one to three years; Tier D, two guaranteed years plus two team options.

4.2 Four-year Tier A or Tier B contracts cannot be traded until [ANNUAL DATE REVIEW: December 15] of their second contract year.

4.3 Players signed before [ANNUAL DATE REVIEW: March 1] are eligible for re-signing under league rules.

4.4 A player's first re-signing adds 1 Unit to the applicable base salary; a second adds 2 Units, with the increment increasing for later re-signings. This penalty follows the player through trades but resets when the player enters free agency. If the same franchise reacquires the player after the first round of the Free Agency Draft, the re-signing penalty does not apply.

4.5 A rookie-scale extension is at base salary without a re-signing penalty. A Tier B player in the final rookie contract year may qualify for a four-year extension.

4.6 Rookie Max Extension: The franchise must be the player's original fantasy drafting franchise; the player must never have been traded or entered free agency; the player must have completed the rookie-scale extension as the second contract; and the player must have ranked in the top 50 in fantasy points per game in any one of the first seven NBA seasons, subject to the league's minimum-games criteria. The third contract may be up to four years at base salary with no re-signing penalty.

4.7 Veteran Super Max: An eligible player must have been All-NBA at some point and have a current salary of at least 3 Units. The contract may be five years, at normal re-signing salary inclusive of accrued penalties. The player cannot be traded during the first three contract years and becomes trade-eligible December 15 of the fourth year.

SECTION 5 — ROOKIE DRAFT & LOTTERY

5.1 The Rookie Draft has two rounds and uses the same order in both rounds (not a snake draft).

5.2 The six non-playoff franchises each choose a distinct NBA lottery team, in this fantasy standings order: fifth place chooses first, then sixth through tenth place.

5.3 The actual NBA lottery ranking among ONLY the six selected NBA teams determines fantasy Rookie Draft positions 1–6. Unselected NBA teams are ignored for this purpose.

5.4 Playoff franchise draft slots: fourth place picks seventh; third place picks eighth; runner-up picks ninth; champion picks tenth.

5.5 Traded picks normally retain the original franchise's draft position. The missed-games penalty described in Section 2 instead applies to the next first-round selection the offending franchise actually makes, including an acquired pick; there is no sixth-pick exemption.

SECTION 6 — FREE AGENCY & WAIVERS

In-Season Free Agency 6.1 In-season free agents are generally acquired first come, first served, subject to Commissioner approval, contract rules, the salary cap and roster limits. Standard free agency closes when the fantasy playoffs begin.

6.2 A franchise releasing a player may not reacquire that player for 30 calendar days. If the player is reacquired by that same franchise during the same season, the original contract remains valid without additional Units.

Wins Pool — Free Agency Draft 6.3 The offseason Free Agency Draft uses a straight, non-snake selection order determined by the NBA Wins Pool. Each franchise receives a slot with three distinct NBA teams; the sum of those teams' actual NBA regular-season wins determines Wins Pool standings. The highest total receives first Free Agency Draft selection.

6.4 Wins Pool slot choice proceeds in REVERSE order of the PREVIOUS NBA Wins Pool standings: the prior last-place finisher selects a slot first and the prior winner selects last. This is not determined by the prior Free Agency Draft order.

6.5 Wins Pool slot chart:

Slot 1: NBA team ranks 1, 20, 30

Slot 2: 2, 19, 29

Slot 3: 3, 16, 28

Slot 4: 4, 17, 27

Slot 5: 5, 15, 26

Slot 6: 6, 13, 24

Slot 7: 7, 11, 25

Slot 8: 8, 18, 21

Slot 9: 9, 14, 23

Slot 10: 10, 12, 22

6.6 Wins Pool ties are broken in this order: fewest missed fantasy games; worse fantasy regular-season record; fewer fantasy points; coin flip. Re-signing decisions and terms are due [ANNUAL DATE REVIEW: August 4]. The Free Agency Draft is scheduled for [ANNUAL DATE REVIEW: September 15], with rosters locked three calendar days beforehand. A franchise at 25 Units may select only eligible G-League or Two-Way players in the Free Agency Draft. Franchises may release players during the Free Agency Draft to create available roster space or salary-cap room. Such releases take effect immediately and must be completed before the franchise makes a selection requiring that space or cap room. All applicable contract, release, and salary-cap rules remain in effect.

10-Day Contracts 6.7 Each franchise receives TWO TOTAL 10-day contract signings per season beginning [ANNUAL DATE REVIEW: January 1]. A second 10-day deal for the same player counts as the second signing. Only Tier C or rookie-eligible players qualify; each deal costs zero Units and requires an available active roster spot. The deal expires at midnight on day ten, at which point the player must be released, signed to a standard contract, or signed to a second 10-day deal if the franchise has an unused signing. These players are ineligible for the playoffs under a 10-day contract.

Waivers 6.8 Beginning [ANNUAL DATE REVIEW: January 1], ALL released players, including buyouts, are subject to a 24-hour waiver claim period. Claim priority follows reverse fantasy regular-season standings, worst record first. Unclaimed players become first come, first served. Standard roster, cap and contract requirements apply; buyouts also follow Section 9.3.

SECTION 7 — TRADES & DRAFT PICK RESTRICTIONS

7.1 Trades may include players, draft picks, dead cap or cap holds, and must comply with applicable roster, salary and contract restrictions. All trades require Commissioner approval.

7.2 Trade deadline: 8:00 PM on the [ANNUAL DATE REVIEW: Sunday following the NBA trade deadline]. The Commissioner shall confirm and publish the applicable date annually.

7.3 Players signed in the offseason Free Agency Draft cannot be traded until [ANNUAL DATE REVIEW: December 15]. Re-signed players may be traded immediately unless another restriction applies.

7.4 Newly Acquired Player Trade Restrictions: Players acquired through free agency after the fantasy season begins cannot be traded for 30 days following their acquisition. Players acquired through a trade may be traded again within their first 30 days only as part of a one-for-one player trade. After 30 days, this restriction expires. All other applicable trade restrictions remain in effect.

7.5 Four-year Tier A/B and Veteran Super Max restrictions also apply as stated in Section 4.

7.6 Teams submit agreed trades and processing times to the Commissioner. Submissions received by 3:00 PM may be processed the same day; later submissions are processed the next calendar day before games.

7.7 Rookie Draft selections may be traded immediately (Wiggins/Love exception to December 15 restrictions); this exception does not apply to players signed in the offseason Free Agency Draft.

7.8 A franchise may trade picks from the current Rookie Draft and five future Rookie Drafts, with one first-round and one second-round selection in each draft, subject to ownership and protection terms.

7.9 Stepien restriction: franchises may not trade away first-round selections in consecutive future Rookie Drafts. Acquired first-round selections count toward compliance.

7.10 Protected pick conditions are freely negotiable between the franchises but MUST be documented in the trade submission. Protected selections do NOT automatically become unprotected the next year. Same-round pick swaps are allowed.

7.11 Missed-games Rookie Draft sanctions follow Sections 2 and 5.

7.12 DeMar DeRozan rule: a franchise that trades away a player may not reacquire that player VIA TRADE for two years.

SECTION 8 — G-LEAGUE & TWO-WAY

8.1 A franchise may carry up to five G-League players and three Two-Way players.

8.2 Eligibility: a player is in the first or second NBA season OR has played fewer than 90 career NBA regular-season games.

8.3 Eligibility is reassessed the NEXT OFFSEASON. A player who crosses the 90-game threshold during a season may remain in the existing G-League or Two-Way position through that season.

8.4 G-League salary does not count against the 25-Unit cap until the player is promoted to the active roster. A player losing G-League eligibility at offseason reassessment may be released without a cap penalty, even if contract years remain.

8.5 Rookie Draft selections are automatically G-League eligible. Free Agency Draft selections may be assigned if they meet the eligibility rule.

8.6 Active roster players may not be demoted or reassigned to the G-League, including during the offseason. An eligible Two-Way player may be reassigned to the G-League during the offseason. Once a G-League player is promoted to active status, that promotion is permanent for the remainder of that season unless the player is released or another rule expressly permits a change.

8.7 Two-Way players are eligible throughout the regular season without a service-day restriction. They may be waived without a cap penalty, converted to standard active contracts under the usual cap and roster requirements, and their Two-Way slot may then be refilled.

8.8 G-League and Two-Way players are not eligible for the playoffs unless promoted before playoff commencement or approved as an injury replacement under Section 10.

SECTION 9 — INJURED RESERVE & SALARY EXCEPTIONS

9.1 Each franchise has two IR slots. IR eligibility follows the ESPN injured reserve designation. IR player salary counts toward the cap. An IR player must be removed within 24 hours of the player's first game back.

9.2 Stretch provision: each franchise may use the stretch provision once every TWO YEARS. The total remaining contract Units are spread over twice the remaining contract years, with a minimum charge of 1 Unit per year. This option is available from opening day until the fantasy playoffs begin.

9.3 Buyout: a franchise may release a player by charging the ENTIRE remaining contract Units to the CURRENT season's 25-Unit cap, provided the total fits. Buyouts are available until the playoffs. Released players are subject to reverse-standings waiver priority, worst record first, as applicable under Section 6.8.

9.4 Disabled Player Exception (DPE): only available if the NBA officially grants a DPE for that player and the fantasy franchise is already at 25/25 Units. The franchise may acquire one free agent or waiver replacement with salary no greater than the disabled player's salary, before fantasy playoffs. The disabled player's salary remains on the cap; the disabled player must remain on IR all season and cannot be traded or removed. A new application is required each season.

9.5 A player's death or retirement may permit exclusion of the remaining contract salary with Commissioner approval.

9.6 Long-term injury relief: clear and undeniable evidence must establish that the player will miss the ENTIRE upcoming fantasy regular season. Commissioner approval is required. The player must occupy IR and remain ineligible throughout that season. Relief equals half the player's salary, rounded UP to a whole Unit, capped at 2 Units per qualifying player. There is no franchise limit on the number of qualifying players.

SECTION 10 — PLAYOFFS & POSTSEASON ELIGIBILITY

10.1 The top four franchises qualify for a two-week postseason. Seeding ties are resolved by head-to-head record, then total fantasy points.

10.2 A tied playoff matchup is decided by the franchise with more points in the PTS scoring category.

10.3 Playoff rosters lock at playoff commencement. Standard free agency closes. G-League and Two-Way players cannot play unless promoted before the playoffs or approved as an injury replacement.

10.4 Playoff injury replacement requests may be submitted starting Sunday of the final regular-season matchup and must be submitted to the Commissioner and approved BEFORE playoffs begin.

10.5 The injured active player must have a verifiable injury designation, short-term or long-term, supported by an NBA injury report or reliable source. Rest, healthy scratches and suspensions do not qualify.

10.6 The replacement must come from the requesting franchise's own G-League or Two-Way roster. Approval requires a simple majority of actual yes/no votes cast by eligible franchises; the requesting franchise does not vote and abstentions are excluded.

10.7 Once replaced, the injured player is permanently removed from that franchise's active playoff roster even if the player recovers. The replacement must remain on the playoff roster for the entire postseason. Roster size, contracts, salary and future rights are unchanged.

10.8 The DPE is governed by the separate timing and eligibility provisions of Section 9.

SECTION 11 — LEAGUE CALENDAR & DEADLINES

11.1 The Commissioner publishes the annual league calendar and may adjust specific dates without a league vote provided underlying rules remain unchanged.

11.1(a) Before each new fantasy season, the Commissioner shall review all specifically dated league deadlines and publish the applicable annual league calendar. Dates may be adjusted at the Commissioner’s discretion without a league vote, provided the underlying rules and eligibility requirements remain unchanged.

11.2 From the start of the NBA playoffs, ALL league activity—including trades, signings, releases and contract changes—is locked until the NBA playoffs conclude AND the ESPN league rolls over. The offseason begins only after both conditions are met.

11.3 The Rookie Draft date is set at Commissioner discretion.

11.4 Annual schedule: contract decisions [ANNUAL DATE REVIEW: August 4]; Free Agency Draft [ANNUAL DATE REVIEW: September 15]; roster lock three calendar days before the Free Agency Draft; offseason Free Agency Draft trade eligibility [ANNUAL DATE REVIEW: December 15]; 10-day contracts and universal waiver period [ANNUAL DATE REVIEW: January 1]; trade deadline [ANNUAL DATE REVIEW: Sunday following the NBA trade deadline] at 8:00 PM; re-signing eligibility cutoff [ANNUAL DATE REVIEW: March 1]. Playoff injury requests begin Sunday of the final regular-season matchup and require approval before playoff commencement.

SECTION 12 — LEAGUE FEES, PENALTIES & PAYOUTS

12.1 Annual League Fees. Each franchise owes $125 annually: a $100 nonrefundable league entry fee and a $25 refundable penalty deposit. The full $125 payment is due by the Rookie Draft. Any unused penalty deposit is refunded at season end.

12.2 Financial Penalties. Each game below the official weekly games limit incurs a $5 fine; each excess game incurs a $5 fine in addition to Section 2 fantasy point penalties. Whenever the $25 penalty deposit is exhausted, regardless of penalty type, it must be replenished to $25 by 12:00 PM Sunday of the following matchup week. Failure results in an automatic loss for that week's matchup. Ten cumulative missed games also triggers the Rookie Draft sanction under Sections 2 and 5.

12.3 Future Draft Pick Deposits. The Commissioner may require a franchise trading a future first-round Rookie Draft pick to prepay 50% of the applicable future season's league entry fee for each affected season. Payment is required before trade processing. Each payment is credited toward that future season's league fees, not added as an extra charge.

12.4 League Payouts. Champion $500; runner-up $250; third $150; fourth $100. All collected financial penalty revenue is divided equally among the four playoff franchises in addition to those payouts.

12.5 Adjustments. League entry fees, penalty deposits and payout amounts may be adjusted at Commissioner discretion or by league vote.

SECTION 13 — COMMISSIONER AUTHORITY & LEAGUE GOVERNANCE

13.1 Commissioner Responsibilities. The Commissioner oversees league operations, enforces the Constitution, approves transactions, resolves disputes and maintains competitive integrity. The Commissioner has final authority over league operations, rule interpretations, amendments and matters not specifically addressed. The Commissioner may consult league members or conduct votes when appropriate.

13.2 League Voting & Rule Changes. The Commissioner has final authority to establish, modify or remove league rules. The Commissioner may seek league discussion or a vote, but is not required to obtain league approval unless another constitutional provision expressly requires it. Votes follow any specific voting requirement for the matter. Otherwise, a simple majority of votes cast determines an advisory vote.

13.3 Competitive Integrity & Tanking. Strategic rebuilding is permitted, including acquiring future picks, prioritizing young players and making long-term roster decisions. Deliberately manipulating standings or draft positions through noncompetitive behavior is prohibited. Examples include intentionally leaving available starting positions empty, deliberately failing weekly games requirements to influence standings, or releasing valuable players to distort competition. The Commissioner may investigate and impose appropriate corrective actions or penalties, and may submit a suspected violation to a league vote at the Commissioner's discretion.

13.4 Disputes & Rule Interpretations. Franchises may bring concerns to the Commissioner. The Commissioner has final authority to interpret rules, resolve disputes and address unprovided-for situations, and may seek member input or conduct an advisory vote.

13.5 Amendments. Franchises may propose amendments. The Commissioner may approve, modify or reject them. Approved amendments are incorporated into the Constitution and communicated to franchises, taking effect on the date established by the Commissioner unless otherwise specified.
